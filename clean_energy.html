Home Quiz Air Quality
Gardens: The plants absorb carbon dioxide, effectively reducing global warming and emitting oxygen, which is essential for humans to live. Moreover, these gardens help reduce visual pollution from the industries and factories being built in the cities.
Expanded info about Gardens.
Use of Electric Vehicles: This will help decrease the burning of fossil fuels, helping reduce the increase in global warming and pollution. Moreover, these non-renewable resources can be sustained for future generations.
Expanded info about Electric Vehicles.
Maintenance of The Home: Due to the roofs of houses, there is a great amount of runoff generated. This runoff can carry waste products from the house, such as oil from leaks, to water bodies, contaminating them. This leads to water pollution.
Expanded info about Home Maintenance.
Recycling and Composting: Helps keep excess waste out of landfills. Moreover, buying products made from recycled materials helps contribute to the effort
Expanded info about Recycling and Composting.
Avoid Aerosols: They contain gases that can contaminate the air and diminish the air quality. This would lead to global warming and acid rain affecting the soil and water as well.
