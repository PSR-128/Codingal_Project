CLEAN ENERGY
Home Quiz Air Quality
Gardens: The plants absorb carbon dioxide, effectively reducing global warming and emitting oxygen, which is essential for humans to live. Moreover, these gardens help reduce visual pollution from the industries and factories being built in the cities.

The air quality has decreased over the years due to the use of fossil fuels and other contributing factors. Along with that, trees, which help remove some of the carbon dioxide in the air, are being removed and forests are becoming deserts. Gardens can be used to combat these problems, as they provide a source of food and habitat for bees and other pollinators, protecting biodiversity. Moreover, they mitigate flooding, so organisms in the area are impacted to a small extent, further protecting species from extinction.
Use of Electric Vehicles: This will help decrease the burning of fossil fuels, helping reduce the increase in global warming and pollution. Moreover, these non-renewable resources can be sustained for future generations.

In the year 2017, vehicles emitted 18.9 million tons of carbon monoxide, according to the U.S. Environmental Protection Agency. Motor vehicles also produce hydrocarbons, nitrogen oxide, and particulate matter that also contribute to air pollution. To solve this, driving more efficiently, driving less, or using electric cars will cut down costs and reduce pollutants emitted annually. Additionally, you can improve mileage by approximately 10 percent if you avoid aggressive driving habits that waste gas, such as speeding, accelerating rapidly, and braking.
Maintenance of The Home: Due to the roofs of houses, there is a great amount of runoff generated. This runoff can carry waste products from the house, such as oil from leaks, to water bodies, contaminating them. This leads to water pollution.

Household products contain volatile organic compounds, which could lead to the depletion of ozone in the ozone layer or other air pollution. To avoid this, products labeled 'Low VOCs' should be bought. Since these products contain a large percentage of VoCs, they can contribute to as much global warming as vehicle emissions. However 'Low VoCs' products can significantly decrease both indoor and outdoor air pollution.
Recycling and Composting: Helps keep excess waste out of landfills. Moreover, buying products made from recycled materials helps contribute to the effort

Mining for new ores requires the processes of extraction, refining, and processing the ores. This costs energy, which could lead to global warming and air pollution. This situation is also the case for many other items, such as paper, plastic, and electronics. Recycling can help reduce the cost of extracting these items for new products. It allows the usage of less energy, allowing the world to combat climate change. Moreover, recycling would help reduce the size of landfills, ensuring safety of the organisms there, as less trash would contaminate their habitat.
Avoid Aerosols: They contain gases that can contaminate the air and diminish the air quality. This would lead to global warming and acid rain affecting the soil and water as well.

Not only do aerosols cause respiratory problems, but they could also trigger allergic reactions, potentially harming a person fatally. Moreover, they deteriorate the Earth's ozone layer, contributing further to increased global temperatures. The escaped chemicals can mix with rain increasing its acidity, causing acid rain, and killing organisms. The substances are slow to decompose, so they stay in the atmosphere for long periods, and are difficult to recycle due to the pressurization, leading to the risk of explosions. Switching to products that can be applied directly can help reduce the amount of chemicals that escape into the air and cause harm.
